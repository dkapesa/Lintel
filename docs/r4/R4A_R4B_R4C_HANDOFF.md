## Locked foundation inherited by R4B

R4B inherits and may not reconsider:

- R4 as the Workspace Product Experience Rebuild;
- the light operational application direction;
- Geist Sans and Geist Mono only in the logged-in application;
- the evidence chain `Change → Observation → Evidence → Requirement → Human Decision`;
- the authority rule `Lintel recommends; the accountable engineer decides`;
- recommendation and Human Decision as separate records;
- the shell `Global Rail → Review Queue → Verification Workspace → Contextual Inspector`;
- Rail areas `Reviews`, `Operations`, `Governance`, `Integrations`, and `System`;
- Queue groups `Needs attention`, `In review`, `Ready`, and `Reviewed`;
- Workspace modes `Overview`, `Change`, `Evidence`, `Requirements`, and `History`;
- the named documentation tokens, reference responsibilities, rejected patterns, product-truth labels, accessibility requirements, and performance boundaries in R4A;
- the canonical workflow and first-five-second understanding contract;
- the prohibition on production implementation before R4C acceptance.

R4B solves interaction architecture inside this system. It does not produce alternate master shells, themes, evidence models, or dashboard concepts.

The laboratory uses representative controlled fixtures to expose these states, and fixture status must remain explicit and must not be described as a production capability. R4C performs responsive, accessibility, keyboard-focus, reduced-motion, and stress validation sufficient to approve the representative laboratory. R4G owns exhaustive testing against the production implementation and final R4 acceptance.

## Authority transfer

External screenshots govern R4A reference responsibility and R4C calibration only. Once the R4C laboratory is accepted:

1. the accepted R4C laboratory becomes the visual authority for production;
2. external products cease governing production decisions;
3. production work must trace visual and interaction decisions to the accepted lab and R4 contracts;
4. future divergence is evaluated against Lintel's accepted system, not by returning to external screenshots;
5. no production implementation starts before this acceptance.

This prevents reference drift, visual averaging, and repeated strategy selection.

## Acceptance gates

### R4A human acceptance

Human review must confirm that the six R4A documents:

- preserve the selected R4 direction without alternatives;
- cover the complete workflow and first-five-second contract;
- assign each reference one responsibility;
- lock usable documentation tokens and shell dimensions;
- separate current capability from planned R4 behaviour;
- preserve recommendation/Human Decision separation;
- define accessibility, responsive, and performance boundaries;
- leave only the named R4B implementation details open.

### R4B acceptance

R4B is accepted only when every open detail has one coherent, testable resolution and every canonical workflow transition has defined state, focus, failure, narrow-layout, and truth behaviour.

### R4C acceptance

R4C is accepted only when the canonical scenario and representative controlled-fixture responsive, accessibility, keyboard-focus, reduced-motion, and stress states visually satisfy the R4A/R4B contracts; the system remains recognisably Lintel; reference influence is bounded; light density is suitable for sustained work; consequential actions remain deliberate; and the laboratory is sufficiently validated to govern production without returning to external references. Exhaustive production validation and final acceptance remain R4G responsibilities.

## R4 final acceptance standard

The completed R4 product must let an accountable engineer enter a real review queue, understand one selected review within five seconds, traverse change through evidence and requirements, understand current and historical movement, detect stale authority, and record a separate Human Decision through an accessible, performant, truthful light workstation. It must preserve equivalent responsibility on narrow and mobile flows and must not imply capabilities, external adoption, or endorsement that the repository cannot substantiate. R4G verifies this standard through exhaustive adversarial production acceptance and freezes R4 after correction of S1–S3 defects only.
