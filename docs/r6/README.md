# R6 — Authenticated Workstation Migration

R6 is the controlled migration of Lintel's authenticated application. It is
separate from, and must not reopen, the frozen R5 public product system.

## Authority

- [R6A — Programme Contract and Baseline](./R6A_PROGRAMME_CONTRACT_AND_BASELINE.md)
  is the accepted and frozen starting authority for subsequent R6 milestones.
  It records current owners,
  protected product truth, migration boundaries, fixtures, capability evidence
  and the R6 acceptance discipline.
- [R6B — Private visual and interaction laboratory evidence](./R6B_PRIVATE_VISUAL_INTERACTION_LAB_EVIDENCE.md)
  records the final independent verdict **ACCEPT R6B WITH NON-BLOCKING
  FOLLOW-UPS**, resolution of blockers A–D, frozen decisions Q1B/Q2A/Q3B,
  measured evidence and later non-blocking follow-ups. It is not production
  authority and does not begin R6C.
- [R6E — Reviews Collection evidence](./R6E_REVIEWS_COLLECTION_EVIDENCE.md)
  records the accepted production Reviews Collection and its final independent
  verdict **ACCEPT R6E WITH NON-BLOCKING FOLLOW-UPS**. It does not claim that
  the R6E freeze commit has occurred.
- [R6F Selected Review foundation and Overview evidence](./R6F_SELECTED_REVIEW_FOUNDATION_OVERVIEW_EVIDENCE.md)
  records the accepted selected Review foundation and Overview, with the final
  independent verdict **ACCEPT R6F WITH NON-BLOCKING FOLLOW-UPS**. It does not
  claim that the R6F freeze commit has occurred.
- [R5 public-system freeze](../r5/R5E2H_FINAL_PUBLIC_SYSTEM_FREEZE.md) remains
  authoritative for every frozen public route and public design decision.

R6 later milestones may change authenticated presentation only within the R6A
contract. They must not create a parallel verification model, recommendation
source, Human Decision ledger, or public redesign.
