## 4. Final blocking corrections

### Verdict and Human Decision integrity

The verdict is now a clause-based flex composition with an auto/minmax identity
row. It has no semantic `max-height`/`overflow: hidden` path. Two clauses are
mandatory wherever a verdict is present:

- `Lintel recommends Tests required`
- `Human Decision pending`

Risk and open/blocking requirement counts are explicitly lower-priority
clauses. The 1440px Overview showed all four clauses. The 1100px Evidence and
1000px Overview checks showed both mandatory clauses in full. The 899px
Workspace showed both in full. Recommendation attribution and accountable
Human Decision remained visibly distinct at every tested width.

### Supporting-left collision contract

The former 88px rail plus 60px glyph Queue has been removed. There is no
single-letter group slicing, generated risk initial, or `font-size: 0`
semantic shorthand.

- Expanded supporting-left: 300px, one shared navigation/Queue plane.
- Manual compact supporting-left: 232px, still a readable Review collection.
- Inspector pressure below the useful three-region threshold: supporting-left
  temporarily yields to 0px, producing Workspace | Inspector.
- Inspector close/sufficient width: the prior manual preference returns.

At 1100px L4, the split measured 0 / 790 / 310px. Closing Inspector restored
the 300px Expanded plane. In L11, closing Inspector restored the 232px compact
plane, proving temporary yielding did not overwrite the manual preference.
